Quantum Computing Basics
========================

Quantum computing is a rapidly-emerging technology that harnesses the laws of quantum mechanics to solve problems too complex for classical computers.

Key Concepts:
1. Qubits: Unlike classical bits which can only be 0 or 1, qubits can exist in a state of superposition.
2. Superposition: This allows qubits to represent 0, 1, or any quantum proportion of both simultaneously.
3. Entanglement: A quantum phenomenon where qubits become linked, such that the state of one instantly influences the state of another, no matter how far apart they are.
4. Quantum Gates: Quantum algorithms use quantum gates to manipulate qubits and perform operations.

Real-World Applications:
- Cryptography: Quantum computers could potentially break current encryption methods but also enable secure quantum cryptography.
- Drug Discovery: Simulating molecular structures at a quantum level to speed up drug development.
- Optimization: Solving complex logistical, financial, and manufacturing optimization problems.
