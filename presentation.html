The Problem

Students struggle to find
quality study material

Good notes and tutorials exist — but they're scattered across WhatsApp groups, random drives, and paid sites that most students can't afford.

  • 📂

    Scattered resources — Notes shared on WhatsApp get lost after a few days.

  • 💸

    Paid barriers — Quality content locked behind subscriptions students can't afford.

  • 🔍

    No search — No organized, searchable place for peer-created study material.

  • 🤖

    No guidance — Students study alone with no interactive help available.

Our Solution

A unified hub for
sharing & learning

LearnHub is a free, community-driven platform where students and teachers upload resources, discover content by subject, and get instant AI-powered help.

🗂️

Resource Library

Upload PDFs, videos, articles, tutorials, notes and links — all in one searchable place.

🤖

AI Tutor

Ask any question and get instant, intelligent answers from a built-in AI tutor.

🔖

Save & Revisit

Bookmark your favourite resources and access them from your personal profile anytime.

🧑‍🏫

Role System

Separate roles for Students, Teachers, and Admins with different levels of access.

🏷️

Subject Filters

Browse by Maths, Science, Programming, Arts, Business and more with one click.

⭐

Featured Content

Admins curate the best resources and feature them on the homepage for everyone.

🔒 Security

Helmet headers, bcrypt password hashing, JWT tokens, rate limiting (100 req/15 min) and CORS whitelist protection.

☁️ Deployment

Frontend deployed on Vercel. Backend hosted separately. File uploads stored server-side with 1-day caching.

User System

Three user roles, one platform

🎓

Student

  • Browse all resources
  • Save / bookmark
  • Use AI Tutor
  • Manage profile & avatar
🧑‍🏫

Teacher

  • All student abilities
  • Upload resources
  • Manage own content
  • Awaits admin approval
⚙️

Admin

  • Full dashboard access
  • Manage all users
  • Feature resources
  • Delete any content

Every account is secured with bcrypt-hashed passwords and stateless JWT authentication.

Key Features

What makes LearnHub stand out

📤

Multi-format Uploads

Supports PDFs, images, video links, articles, tutorials and plain notes — all validated for type and size.

🔎

Search & Filter

Filter resources by subject (Math, Science, Programming…) and type. Jump straight to what you need.

👤

Profile Page

Edit your name, bio and avatar. See your uploaded resources and your saved bookmarks in one place.

🤖

AI Tutor (Protected)

Logged-in users can ask the AI Tutor any academic question and receive intelligent, context-aware answers.

📈

View Counter

Every resource tracks how many times it has been viewed, surfacing the most popular content.

🛠️

Admin Dashboard

Full user management, resource moderation, and homepage curation — all from a dedicated admin panel.
